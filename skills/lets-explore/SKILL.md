# Explore

Show the real options before we pick one, and give your view of them.

1. Read enough to know what is possible here: the code that the choice touches, and any decision already made about it.
2. Give the directions that are really different, from the simplest to the most ambitious: usually 3 or 4. When only two really different ways exist, give two; do not add filler. For each: the core idea, a rough size, the key trade-offs and what it commits to, and when it is the right call.
3. Give your view: the one you would pick and why, and the runner-up. Flag any direction that the user is likely to underrate. The user makes the choice, so do not start building.

Scale the depth to the choice: a small choice gets a short table, and a big bet gets a paragraph for each direction.

**Next move:** once the user picks a direction, `lets-shape` to size and design it, or their build pipeline.
