# Interview

Remove the ambiguity before any implementation.

- Ask **one question at a time**; wait for the answer before the next.
- Prioritize questions where the answer would change the approach or the architecture — skip trivia.
- Cover: the real goal and how you'll know it worked, what's in scope vs out, hard constraints, tricky edge cases, and any references or examples the user already likes.

After ~5–8 questions (or once the ambiguity is gone), summarize what you learned as a short spec and propose next steps for approval.
